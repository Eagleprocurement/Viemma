Part A: Terms and Conditions of Travel

These terms and conditions (the "Terms") govern the relationship between Viemma Tours ("we", "us", "our") and the client ("you", "your", "guest"). By booking a tour with us, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to be bound by these Terms.

1. The Contract and Booking Process

A binding contract is formed once we receive a completed booking form and the required non-refundable deposit. The person signing the booking form guarantees that they have the authority to accept these Terms on behalf of all persons named on the booking form.

2. Payment Terms

  • Deposit: A non-refundable deposit of 30% of the total tour price is required to secure your reservation. For peak season bookings or specific high-end lodges, a higher deposit may be strictly required.
  • Balance: The remaining balance is due no later than 60 days prior to your date of departure. If the booking is made within 60 days of departure, full payment is required immediately.
  • Currency & Fluctuations: Quotes are subject to exchange rate fluctuations. Viemma Tours reserves the right to adjust prices before final payment if drastic currency shifts occur.

3. Cancellations by the Client

Cancellations must be submitted in writing to support@viemmatours.africa. Because of the exclusive nature of our luxury lodge partners and private charters, the following stringent cancellation fees apply:

  • More than 90 days prior to departure: Forfeiture of the 30% deposit.
  • 89 to 60 days prior to departure: 50% of the total tour price is forfeited.
  • 59 days or fewer prior to departure: 100% of the total tour price is forfeited.

No refunds will be given for unused services, including but not limited to missed meals, game drives, or early departures from a tour.

4. Cancellations and Alterations by Viemma Tours

While we endeavor to operate all tours as advertised, we reserve the right to change itineraries or cancel a tour due to Force Majeure (events beyond our control including, but not limited to, extreme weather, political instability, or acts of God). In such events, we will offer comparable alternatives. Viemma Tours cannot be held liable for any incidental expenses incurred by the client (such as non-refundable flights) as a result of these changes.

5. Comprehensive Travel Insurance

It is a mandatory condition of traveling with Viemma Tours that all guests possess comprehensive travel insurance. This insurance must cover medical emergencies, repatriation, cancellation, curtailment, and loss or damage to baggage. Guests will be asked to provide proof of insurance prior to travel.

6. Passports, Visas, and Health Requirements

  • Passports: Passports must be valid for at least six (6) months beyond the intended date of return and contain a minimum of three (3) blank visa pages.
  • Visas: The client is entirely responsible for obtaining the correct visas for all countries visited during the itinerary.
  • Health: Many of our destinations are located in malaria-endemic areas or require Yellow Fever certificates. It is the client’s responsibility to consult a medical professional regarding necessary prophylactics and inoculations.

7. Limitation of Liability and Assumption of Risk

Travel to Africa and the Indian Ocean involves inherent risks, including close proximity to wild animals, travel in remote areas with limited medical facilities, and the use of light aircraft. By booking with Viemma Tours, you accept these risks. Viemma Tours, its directors, employees, and agents act solely as agents for third-party suppliers (airlines, lodges, transport companies) and shall not be held liable for any injury, death, loss, damage, or delay arising from the actions or omissions of these independent suppliers.

Part B: Privacy Policy & Data Protection

This Privacy Policy outlines how Viemma Tours collects, protects, and utilizes your Personal Information in compliance with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) of South Africa and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Information We Collect

To curate and execute your luxury travel experience, we collect the following categories of data:

  • Identity & Contact Data: Full names, passport copies, dates of birth, email addresses, phone numbers, and physical addresses.
  • Special Category Data: Dietary requirements, allergies, mobility issues, or relevant medical conditions. This data is collected strictly to ensure your safety and comfort.
  • Financial Data: Billing details and secure payment processing information.
  • Technical Data: IP addresses, browser types, and website usage data collected via cookies when you interact with our digital platforms.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process your data exclusively for the following purposes:

  • To execute our contract with you (booking flights, securing luxury accommodation, arranging private transfers).
  • To communicate vital itinerary updates, travel advisories, and administrative notices.
  • To ensure emergency protocols can be activated if required on the ground.
  • To comply with legal, regulatory, and tax obligations in South Africa and destination countries.

3. Data Sharing and Third-Party Disclosures

Your Personal Information is treated with the highest level of confidentiality. We only share necessary details with trusted third-party providers (hotels, airlines, ground handlers, and guides) strictly for the purpose of fulfilling your travel itinerary. We ensure our partners maintain equivalent data protection standards. We do not sell, rent, or trade your data to third-party marketing agencies.

4. International Data Transfers

Because your travel spans multiple jurisdictions, your data may be transferred to suppliers operating outside of your home country. We ensure that such transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards and contractual confidentiality clauses.

5. Data Security and Retention

Viemma Tours employs robust digital security protocols, including encryption and secure servers, to prevent unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure of your data. We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, or as legally required by South African revenue and tax laws.

6. Your Legal Rights

Under POPIA and GDPR, you possess the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request the correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Request the erasure of your data (subject to legal retention requirements).
  • Withdraw your consent for direct marketing communications at any time.
